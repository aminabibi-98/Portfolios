01 — Profile

Reading landscapes through data

I'm a geoinformatics student based in Trier, Germany, currently completing an M.Sc. in Geoinformatik. My work sits at the intersection of remote sensing, spatial statistics and environmental science — tracing how forests, cropland and cities shift over decades of satellite observation, and building the tools that make those shifts visible.

Before Trier, I studied Environmental Sciences in Islamabad and worked across environmental data science, science education and international development research in Pakistan. That path shows up in how I work now: I care as much about what a dataset means for people and places as I do about the classification accuracy behind it.
